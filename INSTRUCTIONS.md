# How to Play Hangman

1. One player chooses a secret word or phrase and shows a blank for each letter.
2. The other player guesses one letter at a time.
3. If the letter appears in the word, reveal all of its positions.
4. If the letter is not in the word, add one part to the hangman drawing and record the incorrect guess.
5. Players may guess the entire word at any time. A wrong word guess counts as an incorrect guess.
6. The guesser wins by revealing or correctly guessing the word before the drawing is complete.
7. The word chooser wins if the drawing is completed first.

Do not count repeated guesses more than once.
